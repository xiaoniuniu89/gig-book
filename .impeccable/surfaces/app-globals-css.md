Scope: the whole app shell (home, setlists, tune, tools, bands, landing, error pages). Mode: Operate (landing: Persuade-lite).

## Direction contract
THESIS: The app is a Real Book. Every area wears a Real Book cover: flat neon cover colour, spiral-bound spine, hand-lettered title. Refuses the generic dark SaaS dashboard (navy surfaces, one gold accent, thin left-border cards).
OWN-WORLD: Off-white paper #FAF9F6 and ink #202020. Cover fills: yellow #E0E722 (Gigs), orange #FFAD00 (Practice), fuchsia #DB3EB1 (Niu AI). Red #D22730 for primary pill buttons and active states. Luckiest Guy hand-lettering for titles. Special Elite typewriter for hints, labels and empty states. Lato body. Kalam chord hand. Black stick-figure and silhouette art (Gigle). Dark = stage mode: near-black paper, the same covers, art inverted to white.
STORY: A musician recognises their own book at a glance, finds the gig, and plays. Testers feel the app has a personality that belongs to jazz, not to software.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: hand-lettered "Welcome back, NAME". Three shelves of cover-coloured cards with spiral spines: yellow Gigs (Setlists, Library, Bands), orange Practice (Licks, Resources, Tools), fuchsia Niu AI. Landing: a big hand-lettered headline on the left, the trumpet-and-sax silhouette on the right, then Sign in and Request access side by side.
FORM: Brief-pinned (Gigle, Dan's own site); roll 8ff4c6d7 ran and the user pin overrides it (code-led, no image generation).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
